Evidence, Scientific Trust & Methodology Matrix

This document defines the analytical models, data sources, resolutions, acquisition timestamps, and explicit constraints governing the Lower Godavari Flood Intelligence Prototype.

1. Standardized Scientific Terminology

Approved Terminology Permitted Meaning Strictly Prohibited Misrepresentation
Exploratory Susceptibility Model Intrinsic terrain vulnerability derived via Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP). Identifies where water tends to pond based on physical elevation, slope, and river proximity. Must never be described as a calibrated 2D hydrodynamic flow simulation, real-time flood forecast, or dynamic inundation model.
Potential Inundation Candidates Sentinel-1 SAR pixels exhibiting backscatter below Otsu threshold (≤ -18.42 dB) during the July 16, 2022 flood crest, excluding permanent water and radar shadow. Must never be presented as confirmed flood extent, ground-truth emergency observations, or live satellite monitoring.
Potential Population Exposure WorldPop 2020 statistical raster zonal sum residing within elevated susceptibility polygons. Must never be reported as confirmed victims, displaced persons, casualties, or evacuees.
OSM-Mapped Facilities Community facilities catalogued from OpenStreetMap contributor data. Must never be claimed as verified operational disaster relief shelters, emergency triage centers, or government-staffed posts.
Atmospheric Weather Forecasts Atmospheric precipitation, temperature, and wind predictions proxied from Google Maps Platform Weather API. Must never be conflated with river discharge, stream stage forecasts, or hydrological flood models.
