# Historical Flood Reference: July 2022 Godavari Basin Flood Event

## 1. Overview
In July 2022, the Godavari River witnessed its second-highest flood surge in recorded modern history (surpassed only by the catastrophic 1986 flood). Sustained, intense monsoon depressions over the catchment in Maharashtra, Telangana, and Chhattisgarh produced extraordinary inflows into the Polavaram reservoir and the Dowleswaram Sir Arthur Cotton Barrage.

## 2. Key Hydrological Metrics
- **Peak Discharge**: ~2,580,000 cusecs (~73,056 m³/s) recorded on July 15–16, 2022 at Dowleswaram Barrage.
- **Warning Signals**: Third and highest flood warning signal (red flag) hoisted at Dowleswaram when gauge exceeded 17.75 feet.
- **Upstream Surge**: Bhadrachalam gauge reached 71.30 feet.
- **Primary Delta Branch Submergence**: Both Gautami Godavari and Vasishta Godavari overflowed natural levees, cutting off dozens of island habitations ("Lankas") in the Konaseema delta.

## 3. High-Impact Mandals & Clusters
- **Island / Delta Reach (Konaseema)**: Mummidivaram, P. Gannavaram, Ainavilli, Razole, Kothapeta, Sakhinetipalli.
- **Upstream Gorge / Reservoir Reach**: Devipatnam, Polavaram, Kunavaram, Chintoor, V.R. Puram.
- **Main Plain Habitations**: Outskirts of Rajamahendravaram, Kovvur, Nidadavole, Achanta.

## 4. Benchmark Sentinel-1 SAR Validation Footprints
- Pre-flood baseline: 2022-06-02 and 2022-06-14 (dry season low-flow channel).
- Flood peak: 2022-07-14 00:31 UTC and 2022-07-16 12:43 UTC (maximum inundation extent).
