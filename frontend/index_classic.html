Must never be described as a calibrated 2D hydrodynamic flow simulation, real-time flood forecast, or dynamic inundation model.
Potential Inundation Candidates Sentinel-1 SAR pixels exhibiting backscatter below Otsu threshold (≤ -18.42 dB) during the July 16, 2022 flood crest, excluding permanent water and radar shadow. Atmospheric Weather Forecasts Atmospheric precipitation, temperature, and wind predictions proxied from Google Maps Platform Weather API. Must never be conflated with river discharge, stream stage forecasts, or hydrological flood models.

Academic Research & Geospatial AI Identity

Explainable Geospatial AI for Flood Risk and Impact Assessment with Emergency Decision Support

AI-Based Flood Risk, Impact Assessment and Emergency Decision Support System
Primary Implementation: Andhra Pradesh, India • Detailed Study Basin: Lower Godavari Basin (21 Mandals) • Domain: Disaster Management & Climate Resilience

1. Research Objective & Problem Statement

Tropical river deltas face compound vulnerabilities from monsoon downpours, river surges, and backwater flooding. The Lower Godavari basin frequently handles peak discharge exceeding 2.5 million cusecs at Dowleswaram Barrage.

This project introduces an explainable, multi-source framework synthesizing synthetic aperture radar (SAR) Earth observation, physical terrain hydrology, and demographic exposure with real-time official alerts to provide actionable, human-interpretable emergency guidance.

2. Remote Sensing & Sentinel-1 SAR Methodology

Dual-polarization (VV + VH) C-band Sentinel-1 Ground Range Detected (GRD) data processed across the July 2022 Godavari flood crest:

  • Sensor: Copernicus Sentinel-1A / 1B (SAR C-Band, 5.405 GHz)
  • Orbit: Track 129, Descending pass (16 July 2022 flood crest)
  • Pre-processing: Thermal noise removal, orbit state vector correction, radiometric calibration (sigma nought), Range Doppler terrain correction.
  • Thresholding: Bimodal Otsu thresholding on smoothed VV/VH ratio (-16 dB threshold) yielding 1,087.5 km² of floodwater candidates.

3. Multi-Criteria Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) Weighting

Susceptibility is derived deterministically from physical landscape factors following approved Saaty Analytic Hierarchy Process weights with zero black-box bias:

25% Elevation (DEM) Copernicus GLO-30 DSM
25% River Proximity HydroSHEDS HydroRIVERS
20% Slope Gradient Derived DEM Slope
15% Land Cover ESA WorldCover 2021 10m
15% Historical Hazard Multi-Decadal Frequency

5. Scientific Boundaries & Data Transparency

  • Susceptibility vs Inundation: Susceptibility reflects physical terrain propensity, not guaranteed real-time inundation depth.
  • No Fabricated Depth: Water depth is only presented where calibrated river gauge sensors exist; no synthetic depths are fabricated.
  • Data Attribution: GADM v4.1 administrative boundaries, Copernicus DEM, ESA WorldCover, WorldPop, IMD, CWC, and NDMA SACHET.

Help, Safety Guidance & Flood Preparedness

Practical, verified instructions for citizens, local community volunteers, and emergency responders.

Phase 1: Readiness

Before a Flood Event

  • Prepare an Emergency Kit: 3-day supply of drinking water (3L/person/day), dry ration (flattened rice, biscuits), waterproof torch, portable radio, whistle.
  • Protect Essential Documents: Store Aadhaar cards, property deeds, medical records in a waterproof sealed zip pouch.
  • Know Your Local Shelter: Identify nearest government cyclone/flood relief shelter and highest road route in your mandal.
  • Monitor Official Feeds: Keep notifications enabled on PRAVAH 360 for NDMA and AP-SDMA early warnings.
Phase 2: Life Safety

During Severe Inundation

  • Evacuate Early: Move women, children, elderly, and livestock to designated relief shelters immediately upon receiving District Collectorate advisories.
  • Turn Around, Don't Drown: Never walk or drive into moving water. 15 cm of moving floodwater can sweep adults; 30 cm can float vehicles.
  • Disconnect Electricity: Switch off main circuit breakers and gas cylinders before evacuating to prevent electrocution and fires.
  • Use Emergency SOS: If stranded or injured, trigger the PRAVAH 360 SOS button with GPS coordinates to alert SDRF dispatchers.
Phase 3: Recovery

After Flood Waters Recede

  • Water Safety: Boil all drinking water for at least 1 minute or use chlorine purification tablets before consumption.
  • Structural Caution: Inspect walls and foundation for cracks or erosion before re-entering dwellings. Watch for displaced reptiles/snakes.
  • Food Hygiene: Discard all food items, vegetables, and medicines that came in contact with floodwaters.
  • Report Damage: Log structural damages with Mandal Revenue Inspector for relief package enumeration.
